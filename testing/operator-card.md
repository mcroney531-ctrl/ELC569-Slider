# Persona test

Use a normal chat (ChatGPT or Claude). Not Codex, Agent/Work mode, or any browser-capable mode.

1. Open the tool fresh.
2. Open a normal private chat (ChatGPT Temporary Chat, or a new Claude chat).
3. Paste the test prompt, then the opener below, and attach a screenshot of the landing page.
4. Do what the persona asks in the tool.
5. Send back a screenshot of what happened.
6. Repeat until finished.
7. Save the chat.

During the run:

- Asked a question instead of an action? Answer it normally.
- Asked for something that isn't there? Say: "I don't see that."
- Something takes a while? Wait for it to finish, then send the screenshot.
- If it offers to switch to Work/Agent mode, decline and keep going.

## Opener

```
I'm going to operate the real tool for you. You tell me what Marcus does, one step at a time. I'll send you a screenshot of the result after each action. Don't browse or open the tool yourself.

Here's the starting screen:
```

Run IDs, scoring, and comparison happen afterward, not during the run.
